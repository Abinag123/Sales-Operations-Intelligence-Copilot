Inside the copilot

Three layers, one decision surface

The deployed site is the presentation layer. The Python services in the repository do the scoring, retrieval and generation.

Explainable scoring

A scikit-learn pipeline — median imputation, one-hot encoding, standardization and logistic regression at a 0.75 cut-off — exposed through POST /predict and reproduced exactly in the browser.

Try the scoring lab

Grounded knowledge

Sentence-transformer embeddings in ChromaDB plus a BM25 keyword index, fused with reciprocal rank. Answers cite the exact document chunk they came from.

Ask the knowledge base

Honest reporting

Hold-out metrics, threshold trade-offs, confusion matrix and explicit limitations published next to the demo — evaluated on the reserved 20% split of the dataset.

Retrieval-augmented assistant

Answers with receipts

The assistant retrieves the most relevant chunks from three company documents and asks a local Llama 3.2 model to answer using only that context. Every response ships with the supporting passages.

  • Hybrid retrieval: semantic search + BM25 keyword search
  • Reciprocal rank fusion for a single, stable ranking
  • Explicit "not in the documents" behaviour
  • Discounts and quotations stay behind human approval
