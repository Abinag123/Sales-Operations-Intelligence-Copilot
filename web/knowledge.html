Retrieval pipeline

What happens before the model speaks

  1. Chunk — documents split into ~500-character windows with 100-character overlap.
  2. Embed — all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence embeddings stored in ChromaDB with cosine distance.
  3. Keyword search — a BM25 index over the same chunks catches exact policy terms.
  4. Fuse — reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60) merges both rankings into one list.
  5. Ground — the top three chunks become the only context the LLM may use, with a strict citation instruction.
Policy guardrail: the assistant may recommend follow-up actions, but it must not approve discounts, confirm quotations, or send customer commitments without human approval.
