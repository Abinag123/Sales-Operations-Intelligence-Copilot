Why logistic regression

Small data, mixed feature types, and a need for stable, inspectable coefficients. The pipeline handles imputation, scaling and encoding in one serialized object, and beats a majority-class baseline on the hold-out split.

What drives the score

Budget, website visits, calls and response speed carry the largest standardized weights. Channel and industry shift the log-odds only slightly — consistent with their narrow conversion spread.

Known limits

  • Synthetic training data — no proven business lift.
  • 0.659 ROC-AUC is modest; ranking quality is limited.
  • No time dimension, so drift is unmeasured.
  • Fairness by segment has not been evaluated.
